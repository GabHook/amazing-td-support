Before contacting support

Including the following details helps us locate the right account and resolve your request:

  • your in-game display name, PlayFab ID, or Google Play Games profile name, if available;
  • your device model and Android version;
  • a short description of what happened and the steps that led to it;
  • screenshots of any visible error, without passwords or sensitive payment information; and
  • for a purchase issue, the Google Play order ID, but never full card details.

Support email: go4access@gmail.com

Quick troubleshooting

  • Confirm that Android, Google Play services, and the game are up to date.
  • Check your internet connection before signing in or restoring cloud progress.
  • Confirm that Google Play Games is using the expected Google account.
  • Restart the game and device before reinstalling. Reinstalling can remove local-only data.
  • For purchases, use Google Play’s restore or purchase-history controls and keep the order ID available.
