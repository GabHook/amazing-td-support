AMazing TD - Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how players can request deletion of their AMazing TD account and associated player data. AMazing TD is published by Go4Games.

Request account deletion

How to request deletion

  1. Email go4access@gmail.com with the subject line AMazing TD account deletion request.
  2. Include enough information for us to identify and verify your game account, such as your in-game display name, PlayFab ID if you know it, Google Play Games profile information if used, or the email address linked to the game account.
  3. If your request relates to purchases, include your Google Play order ID or purchase details. Do not send passwords or payment card details.

We may reply to ask for additional verification before deleting account data. Requests are usually handled within 30 days after the account is verified.

Data deleted

When an account deletion request is completed, we delete or anonymize AMazing TD account data that we control, including:

  • PlayFab account and player profile data associated with AMazing TD.
  • Cloud save data, game progress, inventory, currency balances, player statistics, and leaderboard-related game records stored for AMazing TD.
  • In-game display name, avatar/profile data, and account identifiers stored for AMazing TD.
  • Purchase entitlement records stored by AMazing TD where deletion is permitted.

Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained for a limited period where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, accounting, dispute handling, or platform compliance reasons. Support emails related to the deletion request may also be retained as a record of the request.

Google Play purchase history, payment records, subscriptions, Google Play Games data, and Google account data are managed by Google. Active subscriptions must be cancelled through Google Play.

Partial data deletion

You can also contact go4access@gmail.com to request deletion of some AMazing TD data without deleting the full account. Please describe the specific data you want deleted.

Need help finding your account?

Open AMazing TD and send us the display name or account information visible in the game. If you signed in with Google Play Games or email/password, mention that in your request.
