Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Tower Defense: AMazing TD (package com.Go4.AMazing) handles information when you play the game, use connected services, make purchases, or contact support.

Publisher: Go4Games  ·  Last updated: August 23, 2026

Plain-language summary. The game may transmit account, purchase, approximate location, gameplay activity, and device identifier data. It uses this information for game features, accounts and cloud progress, analytics, security, advertising, personalization, and purchase management. Data is encrypted in transit. You may request account and data deletion.

1. Scope

This policy applies to Tower Defense: AMazing TD, its game services, and support requests sent to Go4Games. The Google Play Store, Google Play Games, Google Play Billing, Microsoft PlayFab, advertising services, and this GitHub Pages website are operated by third parties and may process information under their own privacy terms.

3. How data is obtained

  • From you: information you enter, optional profile details, purchases you initiate, and support messages.
  • Automatically: device identifiers, approximate location, game interactions, diagnostics, and service events generated when the app or its connected services run.
  • From platforms and service providers: sign-in identifiers, purchase status, entitlements, and account or game-service records supplied by Google Play or PlayFab.

4. Services, processors, and sharing

We disclose data only as needed to operate the game, provide purchases and connected features, analyze and secure the service, comply with law, or display and measure advertising. Current service roles include:

  • Microsoft PlayFab: account identity, cloud saves, game progress, inventory, virtual currency, statistics, leaderboards, and related game-service operations. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Google Play services, Google Play Games, and Google Play Billing: platform sign-in, profile or user identifiers, purchases, entitlement verification, and other Android/Play functionality. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising and analytics services included in the app: may receive approximate location, device identifiers, app interactions, and other actions for analytics, fraud prevention, advertising, and ad measurement, as reflected in the Google Play Data Safety declaration.
  • Authorities or professional advisers: information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users, investigate fraud or abuse, enforce rights, or handle a legal claim.

We may also transfer relevant records if the game or its business is reorganized, sold, or transferred, subject to applicable law and appropriate notice.

This support site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information such as IP addresses and access logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This static site does not itself use advertising or analytics scripts.

5. Retention and deletion

Account and cloud-game data is generally retained while the associated account remains active and for as long as needed to provide the game. When a verified deletion request is approved, we aim to delete or anonymize the account and associated game data within 30 days.

Some records may be retained longer when required for legal, accounting, tax, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, purchase-verification, or platform-compliance purposes. Deleted information may remain temporarily in restricted backups until those backups are overwritten under normal retention schedules. Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, document the response, and meet legal obligations.

Google controls retention of Google account, Google Play payment, order, subscription, and Play Games platform records. A request to us does not delete data held independently by Google.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, obtain a copy of, restrict, object to the processing of, or delete personal data, and to withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

  • You can avoid supplying optional name, email, or user-generated profile information.
  • You can manage Google account, advertising, and Play Games choices in Android and Google settings where available.
  • You can request full account deletion or deletion of particular game data by following the deletion instructions.

We may ask for information reasonably necessary to locate the account, verify that you control it, and prevent unauthorized deletion. We will not ask for passwords or full payment-card details.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information, limit access, and prevent unauthorized use. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please do not send passwords, full card details, or unrelated sensitive information in support messages.

8. Children

The game is not directed to children. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

9. International processing

Go4Games and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we rely on lawful transfer mechanisms and appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, its service providers, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date above identifies the current version. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or store listing where appropriate.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email go4access@gmail.com. Include “AMazing TD privacy request” in the subject and do not send passwords or full payment-card details.
